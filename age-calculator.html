Age Calculator

Calculate exact age from date of birth in years, months, and days. You also get total days lived, approximate months and next birthday information. This calculator runs entirely in your browser and uses local time for dates.

Client-side only Uses local time No data is stored

Enter your dates

Select your date of birth and an optional "as of" date. If you leave the second row empty, today will be used.

Leave all three fields empty to calculate age as of today.

Tip: For the most accurate result, make sure the dates are valid and the "as of" date is not before the birth date.
